Add the Despia connector to your AI builder, and the builder reads this documentation and your Despia projects, so it writes correct Despia code and answers questions about your app and its builds. Changes to your app, builds and publishing are made in the Despia console.

The connector address:

```text
https://mcp.despia.com/mcp
```

## Add it in one step

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Lovable">
    1. Open **Connectors**, click **+** and choose **MCP server**.
    2. Name it `Despia` and paste the connector address.
    3. Save, then sign in to Despia when asked.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Base44">
    1. Click your workspace name, then **Account preferences**, then **MCP Connections**.
    2. Click **Add custom MCP**, name it `Despia` and paste the connector address.
    3. Click **Test & add**, then sign in to Despia when asked.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bolt">
    1. In the chat box, click **+**, then **Connectors**, then **Manage connectors**.
    2. Add a custom server named `Despia` with the connector address.
    3. Click **Connect**, then sign in to Despia when asked.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Replit">
    1. Go to [replit.com/integrations](https://replit.com/integrations) and find **MCP Servers**.
    2. Click **+ Add MCP server**, name it `Despia` and paste the connector address.
    3. Click **Test & save**, then sign in to Despia when asked.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ChatGPT">
    1. Go to [chatgpt.com/plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **+**, then **Add custom MCP server**.
    2. Name it `Despia` and paste the connector address under **Connection**.
    3. Create it, then sign in to Despia when asked.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Claude Code">
    1. In a terminal, run: `claude mcp add --transport http despia https://mcp.despia.com/mcp`
    2. Start Claude Code and run `/mcp`.
    3. Choose `despia` and sign in to Despia in your browser.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cursor">
    1. Open `.cursor/mcp.json` (or `~/.cursor/mcp.json`).
    2. Add `"despia": { "url": "https://mcp.despia.com/mcp" }` under `mcpServers`.
    3. Open Cursor's settings and sign in to Despia when asked.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Codex">
    1. In a terminal, run: `codex mcp add despia --url https://mcp.despia.com/mcp`
    2. Start Codex.
    3. Sign in to Despia in your browser when asked.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Your builder will ask you to sign in to Despia once. Then ask it, for example: "Using Despia, which native features does my app use, and how did my last build go?"

## What it can do

- Read this documentation.
- Read your Despia projects, their packages and settings, and your builds.

It cannot change your app, start builds or publish. Do those in the Despia console.

## Details

For developers: the Despia connector is an MCP server at `https://mcp.despia.com/mcp` (Streamable HTTP). Sign-in uses OAuth in your browser: you choose the Despia workspace and approve what the builder may do.

| Permission | What it allows |
| :-- | :-- |
| Read | Read your projects, builds, packages and this documentation |

A refused secret answers with the error code `secret_in_chat`. You can disconnect the builder at any time in the Despia console.

## Troubleshooting

- **The builder does not ask you to sign in:** remove the connector and add it again.
- **Sign-in shows the wrong workspace:** sign out of Despia in your browser, then sign in again and choose the workspace.
- **A build or publish step is refused:** the permission is missing. Sign in again and approve it.
