title: "Extensions"

icon: "puzzle-piece"
tag: "Beta"
---

V3 extensions are moving to a V4 DSX subsystem, called from your web app as `window.dsx.module.<chain>.<action>`. Extensions built now carry over to V4, and V3 takes on no new technical debt. This is a beta, and details follow when it ships.
