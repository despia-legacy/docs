  Checked 2026-10-09 against Base44's [Setting up a custom MCP](https://docs.base44.com/documentation/account-and-billing/setting-up-a-custom-mcp) guide. Base44 connects **remote** MCP servers by URL, so it uses the Despia Docs MCP.
</Info>

<Note>
  Custom MCP connections need a Base44 **Builder** plan or higher, and an account can hold up to 20 of them.
</Note>

## Add the Despia Docs MCP

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account preferences">
    Click your workspace name at the bottom left, then **Account preferences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to MCP Connections">
    Under **Builder**, open **MCP Connections** and click **Add custom MCP**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the connection">
    - **Name:** `Despia Docs`
    - **URL:** `https://setup.despia.com/mcp`
    - **Authentication:** **Not required**. Leave custom headers empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and add">
    Click **Test**, then **Test & add**. The connection is available to the AI chat in all your Base44 apps, and you can switch it off at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
